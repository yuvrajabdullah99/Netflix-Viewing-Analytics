Title Correlation Matrix

Which titles get watched by the same subscribers, based on viewing time

How to read this

For every subscriber we totalled how many minutes they spent watching each title (episodes watched × episode length), then measured how closely two titles' viewing time rises and falls together across all subscribers (a Pearson correlation, from −1 to +1). A value near +1 means subscribers who spend more time on one title tend to also spend more time on the other — a strong "watch both" pair. A value near 0 means the two titles' audiences barely overlap in how much time they give each.

Titles are ordered on both axes by genre group (sitcom, K-drama romance, crime & thriller, supernatural) rather than alphabetically, using the genre tags in the source data, so titles that share an audience sit next to each other on the diagonal.
